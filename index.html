How should I store them before baking?+

Keep them refrigerated until you're ready to bake. They stay fresh for up to 10 days in the fridge. You can also freeze them for up to 3 months — just bake straight from frozen, adding 5 extra minutes.

Are they really gluten free?+

Yes! Cheese Bites are made with tapioca flour, which is 100% gluten free. However, they are prepared in a kitchen that may handle wheat products, so we recommend caution if you have a severe gluten allergy.
